A personal publishing tool for the LosPibardos gaming channel.

Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy

What it does

lospibardos_69 is a private tool used only by the owner of the LosPibardos channel (twitch.tv/lospibardos_69). It turns highlights from our own Twitch streams into short vertical videos, publishes them on our YouTube channel and, when the owner chooses, publishes the same videos on our own TikTok account at the same scheduled date and time.

How it works

  1. The owner reviews the finished short videos in a private Telegram chat.
  2. The owner explicitly selects which videos go to TikTok and confirms.
  3. The owner logs in with TikTok and authorizes the app (Login Kit).
  4. At the scheduled time, the tool uploads the selected video with its caption to the owner's TikTok account using the Content Posting API.

It only posts content created by the channel owner, to the owner's own account. It is not offered to other users.
